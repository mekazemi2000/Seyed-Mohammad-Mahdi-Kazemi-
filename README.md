# Dr. Seyed-Mohammad-Mahdi Kazemi

Assistant Professor of Financial Mathematics  
Department of Mathematics, Kharazmi University  

I work at the intersection of **financial mathematics**, **actuarial science**, **machine learning**, **deep learning**, and **risk analytics**. My academic and applied projects focus on data-driven modeling for finance, insurance, quantitative risk management, and actuarial applications.
